## A few useful first steps

1. Confirm where your classes take place before comparing commutes and daily routines.

2. Explore residences, rental resources and the Housing Service through KU Leuven.

3. Before paying or signing, review the listing, costs, contract and check-in arrangements; ask the Housing Service about uncertainties.

## Keep exploring

The official resources below explain current requirements and arrangements. Read the guidance for your own situation; this CSAL guide helps you find the right starting point.
