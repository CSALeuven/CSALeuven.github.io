## A few useful first steps

1. Check your faculty’s orientation programme and note the sessions that apply to you.

2. Follow faculty guidance for Toledo, your individual study programme and course registration.

3. For study or student-life questions, use Stuvo to find appropriate support.

## Keep exploring

The official resources below explain current requirements and arrangements. Read the guidance for your own situation; this CSAL guide helps you find the right starting point.
