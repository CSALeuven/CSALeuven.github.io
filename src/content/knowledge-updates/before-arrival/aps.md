### APS interview language

For the Belgian Dutch-language-region APS procedure, the current APS page specifies English for the interview. Do not transfer German-procedure language options to this route; follow your Belgian procedure’s instructions.

Sources: [APS China](https://www.aps.org.cn/zh/bel).

<!-- NSG-120 -->

### APS applicability and exemptions

APS is not universal for every student going to Belgium. KU Leuven lists full and interview-only exemptions, including a full exemption for CSC scholarship students. Ask the university to confirm your route based on nationality, previous study and admission status.

Sources: [KU Leuven Admissions](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/education/apply/requested-documents/aps-certificate) · [APS China](https://www.aps.org.cn/zh/bel).
