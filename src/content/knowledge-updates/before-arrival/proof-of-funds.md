### KU Leuven blocked-account amount

Checked 14 September 2026: KU Leuven lists €1,100 per month for a single new student’s blocked account from 2026–2027. This university requirement is distinct from the statutory visa minimum. Check the current table and your agreement; retain €1,000 only as the dated 2024–2025 figure.

Sources: [KU Leuven Student Immigration](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/life-at-ku-leuven/immigration-residence/long-stay-visa-type-d-visa-for-stays-longer-than-90-days/students-including-doctoral-scholarship-holders/blocked-account).
