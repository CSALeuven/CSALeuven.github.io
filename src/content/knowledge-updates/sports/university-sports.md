### 2026–2027 sports card

For 2026–2027, the KU Leuven Association student sports card costs €37 and is valid from 1 September 2026 to 30 September 2027. Check the official tariff for other categories and later academic years.

Sources: [KU Leuven Sport](https://www.kuleuven.be/sport/over/tarieven).

<!-- NSG-076 -->

### Univ-fit subscription

As checked on 14 September 2026, the Association-student Fitness & Go subscription costs €160 for 12 months plus a valid sports card; €95 covers six months. See the official tariff for other durations, coaching plans and categories.

Sources: [KU Leuven Sport](https://www.kuleuven.be/sport/over/tarieven).

### University sports activities

KU Leuven Sport's current offer lists more than 80 sports. Check the official activity directory for the available sessions and enrolment conditions.

Sources: [KU Leuven Sport](https://www.kuleuven.be/sport/eng/offer).
