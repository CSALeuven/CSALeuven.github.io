<!-- NSG-039 -->

### Old pink bags

Do not use the old pink-bag category. Use blue PMD bags for eligible plastic packaging and check composite items such as paper cups individually.

Sources: [City of Leuven](https://www.leuven.be/en/waste-bags) · [Fost Plus](https://www.fostplus.be/en/media/395/download).

<!-- NSG-060 -->

### Temporary recycling-park access

Eligible users can request a free temporary recycling-park card valid for three months; waste-disposal charges are separate.

Sources: [City of Leuven](https://leuven.be/tijdelijke-toegangskaart).

<!-- NSG-088 -->

### Coffee capsules

Plastic or aluminium coffee and other drink capsules belong in the blue PMD bag, not GFT.

Sources: [Fost Plus](https://www.fostplus.be/en/blog/capsules-for-coffee-and-other-drinks-now-with-pmd).

<!-- NSG-089 -->

### Tea bags and coffee pads

Do not put tea bags or coffee pads in GFT; distinguish them from loose tea, coffee grounds and paper coffee filters using the current sorting list.

Sources: [OVAM](https://ovam.vlaanderen.be/gft).
