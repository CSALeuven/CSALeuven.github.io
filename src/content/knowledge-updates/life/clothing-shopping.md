### PayPal and return postage

PayPal Belgium Buyer Protection does not reimburse return shipping; check the seller's return rules and your possible costs before ordering.

Sources: [PayPal Belgium](https://www.paypal.com/be/legalhub/paypal/buyer-protection?locale.x=en_BE).
