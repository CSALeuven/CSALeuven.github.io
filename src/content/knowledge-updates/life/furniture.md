### IKEA parcel delivery

### IKEA bulky delivery

IKEA bulky delivery uses weight and service tiers, with front-door delivery currently starting at €39.90; use the quote for your actual order.

Sources: [IKEA Belgium](https://www.ikea.com/be/en/customer-service/services/delivery/).
