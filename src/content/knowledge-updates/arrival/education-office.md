### Returnee certificate discontinued

The Ministry of Education abolished the Certificate for Returned Overseas Students from 1 November 2020; diplomatic missions stopped accepting new applications from 1 October 2020. The handbook’s prerequisite filing statement concerns that discontinued certificate.

Sources: [中华人民共和国教育部](https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_xxgk/s5743/s5744/A20/202009/t20200917_488440.html).
