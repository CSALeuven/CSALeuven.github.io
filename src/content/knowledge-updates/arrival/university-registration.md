### Paying tuition by bank transfer

Pay KU Leuven tuition by bank transfer using the reference on your account balance. Cash and credit-card payments are not accepted. Check your invoice and deadline in KU Loket rather than preparing a VISA card for an in-person payment.

Sources: [KU Leuven Tuition Fees](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/education/student/fees/payment).

<!-- NSG-105 -->

### University Hall registration address

Verified Leuven campus registration location: University Hall, Naamsestraat 22, 3000 Leuven. Follow the registration email for appointments and whether attendance is needed.

Sources: [KU Leuven Registration](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/apply/education/student/register/other-campuses/leuven).
