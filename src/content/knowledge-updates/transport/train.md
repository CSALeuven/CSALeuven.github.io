<!-- NSG-010 -->

### Buy before boarding

Since 1 July 2026, SNCB no longer sells tickets onboard. Obtain a valid ticket before boarding.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/support/customer-service/on-board-pricing).

<!-- NSG-037 -->

### Travelling without a valid ticket

Travel without a valid ticket can result in the current €90 regularisation notice; follow SNCB's notice instead of relying on the old €75 figure.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/support/customer-service/on-board-pricing).

<!-- NSG-038 -->

### Connections and stopovers

A regular ticket does not permit a discretionary stopover. Required connections are different; season tickets have their own conditions.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/support/faq/faq-routes-schedules/faq-choose-your-route).

<!-- NSG-050 -->

### Getting a current train fare

Get a current SNCB quote using your age, route, date and Train+ status instead of the old price range.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/products/adult) · [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/products).

<!-- NSG-051 -->

### Weekend discounts

For adults aged 26–64, the current weekend discount is 30% on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays; the old Friday 19:00 half-price return rule no longer applies.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/products/weekend).

<!-- NSG-052 -->

### Under-26 fares

Under-26 travellers can use current youth fares; do not use the old €6.40 GoPass 1 flat price.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/products/youth).

<!-- NSG-053 -->

### GoPass 10 / Youth Multi discontinued

GoPass 10 / Youth Multi is no longer sold; use the purchase and pre-boarding instructions for your current ticket.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/tickets-and-railcards/go-unlimited?menu=1).

<!-- NSG-054 -->

### Student Multi: card and validation ticket

The current corresponding product is Student Multi. Its SNCB conditions effective 1 July 2026 specify a laminated paper card and validation ticket for five returns on a fixed route within 49 days; another webpage section mentions MOBIB, so confirm the medium and application requirements with SNCB.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/fr/products/abonnements/student-multi?journey=2) · [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/-/media/files/pdf/productfiches/fr/student-multi-fr-ext-20260701.ashx).

<!-- NSG-055 -->

### GoUnlimited / Youth Holidays discontinued

GoUnlimited / Youth Holidays is discontinued; its old weekly and monthly prices cannot be used for current planning.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/tickets-and-railcards/go-unlimited?menu=1).

<!-- NSG-056 -->

### Brussels Airport supplement

The Brussels Airport-Zaventem supplement is currently €6.90 per single journey; first check whether your ticket already includes it.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/tickets-and-railcards/airports/brussels-airport/).

<!-- NSG-057 -->

### Student Multi eligibility

Student Multi has income and work-status conditions as well as age and enrolment requirements; check the current SNCB terms if you receive wages, benefits or self-employment income.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/-/media/files/pdf/productfiches/fr/student-multi-fr-ext-20260701.ashx).

<!-- NSG-103 -->

### S-train coverage

S trains are suburban services around Brussels, Antwerp, Ghent, Liège and Charleroi.

Sources: [SNCB/NMBS](https://www.belgiantrain.be/en/travel-info/train-offer/s-train?journey=list).
