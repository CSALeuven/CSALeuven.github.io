### Taxi ranks

Use the station's Martelarenplein rank or a current city-listed central rank, such as Ferdinand Smoldersplein or Margarethaplein.

Sources: [City of Leuven](https://www.leuven.be/taxi).

<!-- NSG-059 -->

### Checking taxi fares

Do not treat €10–20 as a universal quote. Rank taxis follow the municipal tariff; confirm the trip estimate for other services before boarding.

Sources: [City of Leuven](https://www.leuven.be/taxi).
