### Turning left

Observe and signal before turning, then follow that junction's signals, signs and turning-priority duties; a blanket rule cannot replace them.

Sources: [Local Police](https://www.politie.be/5907/sites/5907/files/media/file/2025-05/Veilig_op_stap.pdf).
