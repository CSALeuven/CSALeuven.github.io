<!-- NSG-035 -->

### Buying and paying for bus tickets

Do not rely on buying a cash ticket from the driver; activate an appropriate ticket before boarding or use supported onboard contactless payment.

Sources: [De Lijn](https://help.delijn.be/hc/en-us/articles/360039272292-Why-can-t-I-pay-with-the-driver) · [De Lijn](https://www.delijn.be/en/content/ticket/).

<!-- NSG-036 -->

### Activation and transfers

Activate a digital ticket before boarding. A single permits transfers within 60 minutes; follow the product's last-vehicle completion rule and scan physical tickets as instructed.

Sources: [De Lijn](https://www.delijn.be/en/content/ticket/).

<!-- NSG-040 -->

### Student Bus Pass price

The Student Bus Pass currently costs €25 per academic year; check eligibility and coverage before applying.

Sources: [KU Leuven](https://www.kuleuven.be/onderwijs/student/inschrijven/attesten/buspasaanvraag.html).

<!-- NSG-041 -->

### Applying after initial registration

Apply during registration or use the university's later online application; follow the current form's deadline.

Sources: [KU Leuven](https://www.kuleuven.be/onderwijs/student/inschrijven/attesten/buspasaanvraag.html) · [KU Leuven](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/life-at-ku-leuven/transportation/how-to-apply-for-a-student-bus-pass-in-leuven).

<!-- NSG-042 -->

### MOBIB card fee and validity

A personal MOBIB card is valid for five years. The card fee is €10.

Sources: [De Lijn](https://www.delijn.be/en/content/vervoerbewijzen/mobib/?vertaling=true).

<!-- NSG-043 -->

### Single and SMS tickets

A single ticket is currently €3; texting DL to 4884 adds a €0.25 SMS service charge. Check phone eligibility before using SMS.

Sources: [De Lijn](https://www.delijn.be/en/content/ticket/).

<!-- NSG-044 -->

### Ten-journey tickets

The current ten-journey ticket costs €22; follow De Lijn's current purchase and activation instructions.

Sources: [De Lijn](https://www.delijn.be/en/content/eshop/).

<!-- NSG-045 -->

### Day tickets

The day ticket for ages 12 and over currently costs €9 and lasts 24 hours after activation; SMS purchase has an extra service fee.

Sources: [De Lijn](https://www.delijn.be/en/content/dagticket/).
