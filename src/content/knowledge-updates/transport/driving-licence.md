### Scooters and other mobility devices

Distinguish powered, unpowered and mobility-assistance devices; do not use the old 6/8/18 km/h bands. Ordinary e-scooters follow current cyclist and device requirements.

Sources: [Local Police](https://www.politie.be/5998/nl/vragen/verkeer/regels-voor-voortbewegingstoestellen).
