### Emergency ambulance charges

Emergency ambulance intervention dispatched through 112 uses an annually adjusted flat charge, not time-based billing. Non-emergency patient transport follows different rules. Check the official current tariff; do not delay emergency help because of cost.

Sources: [Belgian FPS Public Health](https://www.health.belgium.be/nl/themas/gezondheid/dringende-geneeskundige-hulpverlening/actoren-dringende-geneeskundige-hulpverlening/teams-dringende-geneeskundige-hulpverlening).

<!-- NSG-117 -->

### When to call 112

Call 112 when you need emergency assistance from an ambulance or the fire brigade, and follow the operator’s instructions. Difficulty arranging transport alone is not an emergency; contact a GP or local out-of-hours medical service for non-emergency care.

Sources: [Belgian FPS Home Affairs](https://112.be/en/who-and-how-call) · [Belgian FPS Home Affairs](https://112.be/en/how-call/how-call-112).
