### Gardasil 9 reimbursement for ages 19–30

Since June 2026, national health insurance reimbursement for Gardasil 9 has been extended to all young adults aged 19–30. Those who are at least 19 but under 31 when receiving their first dose may qualify for reimbursement for up to three doses of Gardasil 9. After reimbursement, the current patient co-payment is approximately €12.80 per dose, or €8.50 per dose for people entitled to increased reimbursement (verhoogde tegemoetkoming/BIM).

Eligibility note: a prescription meeting the reimbursement rules is required. The general rule for this age group excludes people whose HPV immunisation has already started. Confirm previous vaccination, insurance eligibility and prescription requirements with your doctor, pharmacist or health insurer.

Sources: [BCFI / CBIP (RIZIV reimbursement rules)](https://www.bcfi.be/nl/ampps/134015?cat=b) · [VNZ](https://vnz.be/blog/hpv-vaccin-vanaf-juni-terugbetaald-voor-jongvolwassenen-tot-en-met-30-jaar/).

### Eligibility for Stuvo GP services

Stuvo's GP service in Leuven serves KU Leuven Leuven-campus and LUCA Lemmens-campus students. Other students should check their own institution's services or consult a local GP.

Sources: [KU Leuven Stuvo](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/stuvo/health/general-practitioners).

### Mental-health and psychiatric support

Use Stuvo's mental-health portal to identify suitable support. Psychiatric assessment or medication discussions normally begin with a psychologist who can consider referral; check the relevant eligibility and appointment instructions.

Sources: [KU Leuven Stuvo](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/stuvo/health/mental-health/who-can-help-you-with).
