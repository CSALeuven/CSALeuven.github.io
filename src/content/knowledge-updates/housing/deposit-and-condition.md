<!-- NSG-021 -->

### Paying a rental deposit

For a tenancy covered by Flemish student-rental rules, pay a monetary deposit into a blocked account in the tenant's name or the landlord's account. Cash handover is prohibited; retain payment evidence.

Sources: [Flemish Government — Wonen in Vlaanderen](https://www.vlaanderen.be/bouwen-wonen-en-energie/huren-en-verhuren/studentenhuisvesting-huren-of-verhuren/studentenhuurovereenkomsten).

<!-- NSG-022 -->

### Landlord access and consent

A landlord normally needs the tenant's consent to enter; advance notice alone does not authorise entry. Force majeure is an exception. Arrange inspection, urgent-work and reletting visits in advance, with reasonable cooperation.

Sources: [KU Leuven Stuvo](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/life-at-ku-leuven/housing/info-for-tenants/common-mistakes-in-a-student-tenancy-agreement).

<!-- NSG-030 -->

### Deposit deductions and normal wear

Deposit deductions need a basis such as attributable damage or arrears. Wear caused by normal use or age is not tenant damage. Compare and keep the agreed check-in and check-out condition reports.

Sources: [KU Leuven Stuvo](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/stuvo/life-at-ku-leuven/housing/info-for-tenants/inventory-of-fixtures).

<!-- NSG-031 -->

### Deposit return and release

For a tenancy covered by Flemish student-rental rules, a deposit paid to the landlord is usually returned within three months after the lease ends. For a blocked account, ask the bank to release the deposit once you receive the landlord's written statement.

Sources: [KU Leuven Stuvo](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/education/life-at-ku-leuven/housing/info-for-tenants/deposit).
