### Checking the current kotlabel

Since 1 January 2025, check the digital Flemish kotlabel; old blue or green signs are no longer valid certification. The Leuven label additionally covers second-residence tax requirements. Check labels through the city and search housing through Kotwijs.

Sources: [KU Leuven Stuvo](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/apply/life-at-ku-leuven/housing/students/student-housing-in-leuven/kotlabel).
