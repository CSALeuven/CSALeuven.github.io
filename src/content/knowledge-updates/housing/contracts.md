### Landlord access and consent

A landlord normally needs the tenant's consent to enter; advance notice alone does not authorise entry. Force majeure is an exception. Arrange inspection, urgent-work and reletting visits in advance, with reasonable cooperation.

Sources: [KU Leuven Stuvo](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/life-at-ku-leuven/housing/info-for-tenants/common-mistakes-in-a-student-tenancy-agreement).

<!-- NSG-126 -->

### Understanding a Dutch-language lease

KU Leuven recommends signing a Dutch tenancy agreement, with an English translation to explain its terms. Ask Stuvo or a qualified legal adviser to assess an existing English agreement, registration use or court-translation requirements individually.

Sources: [KU Leuven Stuvo](https://www.kuleuven.be/english/life-at-ku-leuven/housing/info-for-tenants/contracts-current).
