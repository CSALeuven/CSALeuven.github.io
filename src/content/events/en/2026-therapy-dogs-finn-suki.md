## A little time with Finn and Suki

On the afternoon of **11 June 2026**, CSAL and Pangaea organised a free therapy-dog activity for students during the exam season. Owners **Rudi and Kelly** brought **Finn and Suki** to Pangaea for a relaxed afternoon together.

The activity was divided into **two sessions of approximately one hour each**, giving students a chance to spend time with the dogs between revision and exams.

## Sitting together and sharing a smile

The first session was open to international students, with students from Agora and Pangaea students who had signed up in advance taking part. Everyone sat around the dogs, petting and playing with them. Finn and Suki also showed off high-fives and hugs, bringing plenty of laughter to the room.

The second session was for Chinese students who had registered in advance, with **15 places available**. Participants exchanged high-fives and hugs with the dogs and enjoyed a short break from the pressures of studying.

## Until we meet again

Thank you to everyone who signed up and attended, and a special thank-you to Rudi, Kelly, Finn and Suki for their company. We look forward to seeing you at future CSAL activities.
