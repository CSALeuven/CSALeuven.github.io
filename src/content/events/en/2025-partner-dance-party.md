## Event recap

On 16 November, CSAL held a partner dance party in Leuven, bringing together students from Leuven and universities in other Belgian cities. Music and dancing gave participants a shared way to meet and get to know one another.

## What happened

Student dance leaders demonstrated the steps before participants were randomly paired for practice. The lessons covered waltz, jazz and disco, with time to get comfortable with the movements, follow the music and work with a partner.

After the dancing, participants stayed to talk about their studies and life in Belgium. CSAL provided free drinks and sparkling water while conversations continued and new connections formed.
