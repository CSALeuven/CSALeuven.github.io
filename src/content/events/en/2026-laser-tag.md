## Gathering for the challenge

At 14:00 on 17 October 2026, CSAL’s Laser Tag activity got under way at LASER3000. A total of 28 students joined the event, gathering and chatting in the venue’s cyberpunk-style corridor before the games.

Participants were divided into two batches, with each batch split into two opposing teams.

## Learning the rules and getting equipped

A staff member explained the game rules, and everyone watched the instructional video to learn how to use the laser guns. Participants then put on their illuminated vests, with red and green lights setting the mood for the battles ahead.

## Tactics, teamwork and shared fun

Inside the arena, intersecting lights, obstacles and cover set the scene for the live-action games. In Tactical Team Mode, participants worked together, moving between cover, dodging and aiming as they put their speed and strategy to the test.

The laser-tag battles were also a chance to practise teamwork. Thank you to everyone who took part. We hope you left with happy memories and new friendships, and look forward to seeing you at future CSAL activities!
