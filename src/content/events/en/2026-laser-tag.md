## Four teams, one tactical challenge

Ready for a live-action laser-tag battle? Join CSAL at **LASER3000** for an afternoon of speed, strategy and teamwork. First-time players and experienced gamers are all welcome!

- **Date:** Saturday, 17 October 2026.
- **Meet at:** 14:00. Please arrive on time.
- **Activity:** 14:00–15:15, with check-in, team allocation and a briefing during the first 15 minutes.
- **Venue:** LASER3000, Bondgenotenlaan 122, 3000 Leuven.
- **Places:** 32, first come, first served, until full.

## Tactical Team Mode and schedule

The 32 participants will form **four teams of eight**. Two teams compete in each match in **Tactical Team Mode**. **Everyone plays two 15-minute games**, with time to rest and chat between matches.

- **14:00–14:15:** Check-in, team allocation and briefing.
- **14:15–14:30:** Match 1, Team A vs Team B.
- **14:30–14:45:** Match 2, Team C vs Team D.
- **14:45–15:00:** Match 3, the teams that lost their first match.
- **15:00–15:15:** Match 4, the teams that won their first match.

## What the fee includes

With a 50% CSAL subsidy, the fee is **€12.50 per person**, including:

- Two team laser-tag games.
- One drink per person: a soft drink, beer or Cava; Cava is shared at one bottle per five people.
- Complimentary nut snacks during the activity.
- Venue and game equipment use.
