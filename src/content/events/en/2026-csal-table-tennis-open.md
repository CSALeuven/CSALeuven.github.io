## Event recap

CSAL held the 2026 CSAL Table Tennis Open on 8 March 2026, bringing together 46 players from three countries and across Belgium.

## What happened

The tournament began with round-robin matches in ten groups. The top four players in each group progressed: the first two entered the Division A knockout bracket, while the next two entered Division B. In total, 40 players advanced. When the competition reached the semifinals, matches changed from best of three to best of five, with spectators gathering beside the tables to watch.

In the Division A final, David Henkens took the first game before Tony Hou levelled the score. Henkens then won the next two games to take the final 3–1.

## Tournament results

| Place | Division A | Division B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Champion | David Henkens | Chenhua Wen |
| Runner-up | Tony Hou | Jiahua Tang |
| Third place | Minghao Jia | Peihang An |

The event closed with an awards ceremony, where CSAL staff presented medals and prizes. Congratulations to the winners, and thanks to the students and staff who helped organize the tournament.
