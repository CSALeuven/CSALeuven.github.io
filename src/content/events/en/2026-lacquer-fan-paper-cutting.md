## Event recap

On 30 March, CSAL held a lacquer fan and paper-cutting workshop at Pangaea in Leuven. The event introduced students to traditional crafts through hands-on activities.

## What happened

Lacquer fan making was a popular part of the workshop. Participants chose their fans, added lacquer to water, then turned and dipped the fans to transfer the flowing colours onto the surface and create their own patterns.

CSAL thanked LOKO for sponsoring this event.
