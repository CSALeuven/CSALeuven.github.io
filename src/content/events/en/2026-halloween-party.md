## Welcome to a sleepless Eastern night

On **30 October 2026**, CSAL hosted its Halloween party, bringing around 60 students together with the organising team for an evening of costumes, sweets and laughter.

The “Meng Po Refreshment Station” offered themed drinks and snacks. To earn the special sweets, participants took on challenges at the different game stations.

## Challenges, sweets and surprises

In the hidden-character challenge, three NPCs mingled with the crowd: participants had to identify them and complete their tasks. A folklore quiz invited everyone to separate fact from fiction in stories about Chinese and Western ghosts and monsters.

Other stations tested quick reactions, choices, spot-the-difference skills and throwing accuracy. Each game awarded a different number of sweets. Collecting 14 sweets earned a mystery gift, and a prize draw added another surprise to the evening.

## Creating together and capturing the moment

The “Soul Workshop” offered a quieter space for drawing, collaborative creativity and photos between games. The evening ended with a group photograph, capturing a warm celebration together far from home.

Thank you to everyone who joined us and to the CSAL team for their work behind the scenes. We look forward to seeing you at another activity soon!
