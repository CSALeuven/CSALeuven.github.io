## Messages and games

Participants shared small joys, stories of overcoming difficulties and encouragement for strangers on an interactive wall, and responded to anonymous messages.

Mahjong, Guandan and board games brought energy to the room, with experienced mahjong players explaining the rules to newcomers.

## Crochet and connection

With guidance, beginners learned to crochet granny squares. Refreshments were available, and some students travelled from campuses outside Leuven to attend.
