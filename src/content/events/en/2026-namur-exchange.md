## Event recap

On 28 March, a group from CSAL visited Namur to meet students from the Namur association. After gathering at the railway station, they explored the city together, stopping for group photos at a square and a bridge over the Meuse while sharing experiences of studying and living in Belgium.

## What happened

Over lunch, the Namur association presented local gifts in return, and the two groups discussed everyday life and running their WeChat accounts. They then visited a chemistry laboratory at the University of Namur, where an on-site introduction offered a closer look at the research environment and work taking place there.

The day combined a city visit with an academic exchange, giving students from both associations time to get to know one another and share their experiences.
