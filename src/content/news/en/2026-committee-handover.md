## A new team takes over

On the afternoon of **16 October 2026**, CSAL held its committee handover at **Leercentrum AGORA (AGOR 114-01)**. Members voted to form the new team, beginning another chapter of service and exchange.

Meet the ten members of the new team on the [About page](/en/about/#current-committee).

## Thank you for being part of CSAL

We thank the outgoing members for their work on activities, student support and cultural exchange, and everyone who has taken part in or supported CSAL. The new team will continue supporting Chinese students and scholars in Leuven and creating opportunities to meet and connect.

## Get involved

CSAL continues to welcome new volunteers for activity organisation, communications, external relations and academic exchange. If you would like to take part, [contact CSAL](/en/contact/) to learn more.

We look forward to making life in Leuven more enjoyable together!
