## Before departure

The embassy advises learning about local security, laws and customs, checking passports, visas and admission documents, and keeping copies. Review entry requirements, including restrictions on belongings, medicines and cash.

## Entry and residence

Understand Belgium’s entry and residence requirements and keep documents valid. During entry checks, remain calm, answer truthfully and provide supporting documents. If treated unfairly, retain evidence and seek lawful ways to protect your rights.

## Housing and rental risks

Use reputable platforms, verify the property, landlord, agent and fees, and inspect the home before signing. Understand deposit returns, repairs, ending or renewing the lease, and breach-of-contract terms. Do not rely on verbal promises; seek legal help when needed.

## Telecom fraud

Be alert to calls spoofing embassy numbers and applying pressure over alleged criminal cases or passport and visa problems. Do not disclose personal information casually, send money or share verification codes. The notice also points to anti-fraud resources from the embassy and China’s consular service channels.

## Currency-exchange fraud

Use banks or other lawful, regulated channels. Do not trust or circulate private exchange offers simply because they come through acquaintances. The embassy warns of financial loss, exposure of personal information and legal risks involving suspected money laundering.

## Everyday safety and getting help

- **Travel and belongings:** Monitor local security conditions, avoid unsafe areas, going out alone at night and demonstrations. Follow traffic rules, wear a seat belt and safeguard belongings. Stay alert at busy stations and travel with others where possible.
- **Physical and mental health:** Pay attention to food and nutrition, address stress and stay in touch with family and friends. Seek medical or mental-health support when needed.
- **Settling in:** Follow local laws and university rules, respect local customs, learn your surroundings and join university or student-association activities.
- **Emergencies:** Stay calm, take appropriate immediate action and seek help from local emergency services or consular protection channels. The original notice lists emergency, consular and student-affairs contacts; use the source link above to consult them.
