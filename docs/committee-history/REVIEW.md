## Confidence and membership boundaries

All 58 names are high-confidence text transcriptions. There are **no unreadable or ambiguous characters**, no guessed image-only names and no duplicates within a year. The rendered article text and decoded `content_noencode` match for every source after layout whitespace is removed. Roster headings, adjacent profile blocks and the introductory election/team context were checked; generic Chinese-name matching was not used. No source JavaScript was executed. No personal photographs were downloaded or copied.

- **2019:** eight names appear in the new-roster profile section. The collective thanks to the outgoing team adds no people.
- **2020:** nine names appear in the new-roster profile section. 吴婧苑 and 麻宏阳 also appear in credits, but are retained once because each independently appears in the roster. A stray “2019-2020” academic-year sentence conflicts with the title and explicit 2020 election date; the canonical archive label is 2020, without an invented term range.
- **2021:** four people are explicitly counted and introduced as the election's announced team, including 高千童 in the associated LMCF section. The later recruitment announcement adds no confirmed people. The credit “Liyi” is excluded as a credit-only alias; no identity mapping is assumed.
- **2022:** fourteen people are explicitly counted and introduced as the announced team, including 吴立芬 in the associated LMCF section. The characters 辛 / 欣 / 然 are adjacent styled spans of one name, 辛欣然; 张 钰 雯 contains decorative spacing. Only layout whitespace was removed; no characters were changed. Repeated names inside introductions are deduplicated within the year.
- **2023:** six named roster profiles precede the recruitment notice. The notice invites future applicants but confirms no additional people.
- **2024:** the introduction states nine staff members and is followed by nine roster profiles. “Andy” is only thanked as a predecessor; “davy” appears only as the article editor. Neither is added or mapped to a Chinese name.
- **2025:** the requested scope is the announcement's **committee/team roster**, not elected office holders alone. The eight names include 洪艺萍 and 邢孝南, each explicitly introduced in the team's trainee profile section. This is evidence of their announced team participation, not a claim that they held elected office or full formal committee status. They precede a separate support section whose people are expressly described as nonmembers. Review this team-scope choice alongside the names before merging. No trainee/role label appears on the public page.

## Public data and presentation

`src/data/committees.ts` is the sole public data source. Every member is a plain Chinese name string; the only committee metadata are year, current flag and original source title/link. Public components do not receive roles, departments, biographies, courses, degrees, portraits, personal contacts, social links or quotations. The original source link is provenance only. Rich source bodies and extraction evidence stay outside the site checkout; this review records only the minimum rationale needed to check inclusion.

The About page replaces its old team placeholder with a prominent 2025 name grid and native, initially collapsed disclosures for 2024 through 2019. Both languages use exactly the same Chinese strings. The old profile-capable placeholder component and data are removed. The surrounding About design, branding, event archive and New Student content are preserved.

Validation is recorded in `VALIDATION.md` after the build and browser review. `source-audit.json` records names and source hashes for future checking without retaining personal profiles.
